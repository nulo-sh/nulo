Clearer sends and app approvals, a recovery phrase check for new wallets, and a new default Testnet node.

- Testnet connects through a new default node. A wallet still on the old default moves to it at its next unlock; a node address you set yourself stays.
- Send: the token picker lists what you hold and adds a token from a pasted address, a zero balance reads 0, sends in progress show in a toast and in History, and a refusal shows beside the balance.
- App approval windows list what leaves your account with the fee, say which calls Nulo cannot check, and name another of your accounts when a send uses its authorization. A failed fee or authorization preview can be retried, and a fee estimate waits behind a send in progress instead of failing.
- Setting up a wallet checks three words of the recovery phrase before Ready, and the phrase step closes itself after 5 minutes.
- The lock screen offers "Forgot your password?" or "Lost your passkey?", and a full backup downloads protected in one step.
- Receive and the Send review show the whole address, and Receive names the network.
- Long forms show a round "more below" button.
- The token list refreshes about every six hours, and on request in Settings → Tokens. Dollar values come from the signed list, so a token it prices shows one without a wallet update.
- The sponsored fee payer is offered only on Testnet and the local network.
- Also: a Switch button on each network in Manage networks, a password hint that judges how guessable a password is, ten new glossary entries, and a question before auto-lock is set to never.
- The Privacy Policy (1.2.9) describes the token list's refresh and the price requests. Nothing needs accepting again.
- Over 60 fixes across send, fees, app windows, onboarding, backup, import and storage.
