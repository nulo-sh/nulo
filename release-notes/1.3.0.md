Send starts from the person you pay, approval windows name every step, and Alpha and Testnet gain a second endpoint.

- Alpha and Testnet gain Nulo's relay as a second endpoint: if a network's first endpoint fails, Nulo uses the next one until the first answers again. A wallet on the default node gets the relay at its next unlock; a node you replaced is left as it is.
- A default network still named Alpha V6 or Mainnet becomes Alpha at your next unlock, unless that name is taken.
- Home shows a What's new row once after this update, and Settings → About links these notes.
- Send starts with the person you pay, then one form with the fee above Review send. Every send opens a review: the recipient, the amount in dollars, what the send makes public and the fee. Fees and Fee Juice balances show dollars, and a fee that would name your account offers private Fee Juice instead.
- A raised fee shows the old figure struck through, and an earlier send's toast steps aside during a review.
- App approval windows number a send's calls as steps with their own details, and show arguments field by field, raw values one press away. Contracts, tokens and the app are named chips, sealed when Nulo verified them; your contacts and accounts appear by name.
- Approval windows name the app's failing check, say who pays the fee and refuse up front what Nulo cannot send without an account. If two balance reads disagree, Nulo reads again, then warns rather than refusing.
- Token rows, Send, History and approval windows draw each listed token's picture, History rows draw their direction, and list amounts follow one rounding rule.
- The connect window says what Allow reveals. Allow every site, there or in Settings → Connected Apps, lets any site learn that Nulo is installed without asking; it starts off, and connecting and sharing an account still ask.
- Connected Apps shows each app's site, and an app's page lists its permissions per contract, as its request did, with a legend for the icons.
- An app that asks while Nulo is locked opens an unlock window that goes on to the connect window.
- Developer → Account State: an Authwits revoke shows in History, rows name their action and contract, and the page drops authwits already used or revoked. Contracts names each contract, with both ids and an explorer link.
- On Alpha, the links to get Fee Juice open Shield; other networks keep Unleashed.
- Also: Settings → Networks shows endpoint order and status, with drag to reorder; the "more below" button reaches the Terms sheet, approval windows and onboarding; Developer mode saves a failed request's logs; every switch has a spoken name.
- The Terms (1.2.4) and the Privacy Policy (1.3.1) describe Nulo's relay, endpoint failover and Allow every site. Nothing needs accepting again.
- Over 30 fixes across send, fees, approval windows, authwits, tokens, networks and restore.
