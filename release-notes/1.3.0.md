Send starts from the person you pay, approval windows name every step, and Alpha and Testnet gain a second endpoint.

- Alpha and Testnet gain a second endpoint, Nulo's relay, run by the Developer: Nulo uses it while the endpoints before it fail, until the first passes its checks again, and a send it carried is checked there until final. A wallet on the default node gets it at its next unlock; a node you replaced is left as it is.
- A default network still named Alpha V6 or Mainnet becomes Alpha at your next unlock, unless that name is taken.
- After this update Home shows a What's new row until you dismiss it; Settings → About links these notes.
- Send starts with the person you pay, then one form with the fee above Review send. Every send opens a review: the recipient, the amount, what the send makes public and the fee. Amounts, fees and Fee Juice balances show dollars where Nulo shows a price, and on a private send a fee that would name your account offers private Fee Juice: pay from it, or get some.
- A raised fee shows the old figure struck through, and an earlier send's toast steps aside during a review.
- App approval windows number a send's calls as steps, each with its arguments field by field and raw values one press away. Contracts, tokens and the app are named chips, with a seal on a contract Nulo knows; your contacts and accounts appear by name.
- Approval windows name the app's failing check and refuse up front what Nulo cannot send without an account. If two balance reads disagree, Nulo reads again, then warns rather than refusing.
- Listed tokens show their picture across the wallet, History rows draw their direction, and list amounts follow one rounding rule.
- The connect window says what Allow reveals. Allow every site, there or in Settings → Connected Apps, lets any site learn that Nulo is installed, and its version, without asking; it starts off, and connecting and sharing an account still ask.
- Connected Apps shows each app's site, and an app's page lists its permissions per contract, as its request did, with an icon legend.
- When an app looks for Nulo while it is locked, an unlock window opens and, once you unlock, carries on with the app's request.
- Developer → Account State: an Authwits revoke shows in History, and opening the page drops authwits the chain says are used or revoked. Contracts rows show both ids and, where it applies, an explorer link.
- On Alpha, the links to get Fee Juice open Shield; other networks keep Unleashed.
- Also: Settings → Networks shows endpoint order and status, with drag to reorder; the "more below" button reaches the Terms sheet, approval windows and onboarding; Developer mode saves a failed request's logs.
- The Terms (1.2.4) and the Privacy Policy (1.4) describe Nulo's relay, endpoint failover, Allow every site and the Shield link. Nothing needs accepting again.
- Over 30 fixes across send, fees, approvals, authwits, tokens, networks and restore.
