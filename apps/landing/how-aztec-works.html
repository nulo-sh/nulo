How Aztec works.

					

						Balances are private unless you make them public. Each transaction is proved on this computer and pays a
						small fee in Fee Juice.
					

					

						

							

								

									
									
								

								

								

									
									
									
								

								
							

							

								
01 Private balances

								

									Your private balance: only you can see it. Your public balance: anyone can see it, like a balance on
									Ethereum. Nulo labels each one, so you always know which is which.
								

								

									Transcript
									

										On most blockchains, anyone who knows an address can read its balance and every
										amount it paid.
									

									

										Nulo shows two balances for each token. Your private balance: only you can see
										it. Your public balance: anyone can see it, like a balance on Ethereum.
									

									

										Your private balance is a set of encrypted notes, like cash in sealed envelopes.
										The network keeps a fingerprint of each note, and a sealed copy only its owner
										can open.
									

									

										Say you pay 3.50 from a note worth 5. Nulo spends that note and makes new ones:
										3.50 for the person you pay, and 1.50 change for you. In a private payment, the
										record shows that something was spent. It does not show which note, or how much.
									

									

										Moving money to your public balance is your choice, and the public side records
										the amount. Nulo labels each balance, so you always know which is which.
									

								

							

						

						

							

								

									
									
								

								

								

									
									
									
								

								
							

							

								
02 Proofs

								

									Your computer proves the private part of a transaction without revealing it, and the network checks the
									proof instead of your private data. Anything done in public stays visible. Proving is the slow step
									before sending.
								

								

									Transcript
									

										When you send on Aztec, the private part of the work happens on your computer,
										not on the network.
									

									

										Nulo runs it right here, with your keys and your notes. Your keys, and what is
										in your notes, stay on your computer.
									

									

										Then your computer builds a proof: math that shows this private part follows the
										rules, without showing what is in it.
									

									

										The proof goes to the network, with sealed notes for the people you pay. The
										network checks the proof, and if it holds, the transaction joins a block. It
										never needed your private data to trust it.
									

									

										Anything a transaction does in public, the network runs in the open, for
										everyone to see.
									

									
Later, one proof for a whole batch of blocks is checked on Ethereum.

									
Building a proof takes real work, so it is the slow step before sending.

								

							

						

						

							

								

									
									
								

								

								

									
									
									
								

								
							

							

								
03 Fees

								

									Every transaction pays a network fee in Fee Juice, the token that pays network fees on Aztec. Pay with
									public Fee Juice and the record names you as the payer. Pay with private Fee Juice and it names a payer
									many people share.
								

								

									Transcript
									
Every Aztec transaction pays a network fee, the way Ethereum charges gas.

									

										The fee is paid in Fee Juice, the token that pays network fees on Aztec. You
										bring it over from Ethereum, and it is used only for fees.
									

									

										The price depends on the work: publishing the transaction's data, and running it
										on the network. Nulo shows you the fee before you send.
									

									

										How you pay matters. Pay with public Fee Juice, and the record names you as the
										payer, each time. Pay with private Fee Juice, and the payer on record is a
										contract many people share.
									

									
Sometimes the fee is sponsored: someone else pays it for you.
