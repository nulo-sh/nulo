# quality-execution-1 phase 5: one confirm list in the execute window

- Pin before the move, in `execute/index.test.ts`, with every other hold cleared (an app-paid operation whose payer read short but could still fund): a processing error disables Confirm, a warning does not. Green on the base.
- The move: one `confirmBlocked` computed in `execute/index.vue` holds every hold but the fee pick; the footer disables on `confirmBlocked || needsFeeSelection`, exactly today's template list; `approve()` returns on `isLoading || refusal || confirmBlocked`, then keeps its fee-pick warning, now read from `needsFeeSelection` rather than a second copy of its expression. `awaitsGrantDecode(op, decoded)` in `display-calls.ts` is the one grant-pending rule; the execute window passes `decodedGrants.has(i)`, the fee window `!!decodedGrants`. The fee window's own confirm list is out of the issue and stays.
- After the move, one more pin: `approve()` called directly sends nothing under a processing error and sends under a warning. Red proof: `!!processingError.value` in place of the `type === "error"` test failed both warning cases; restored from a copy.
- The two direct-call differences, unreachable from the window because Confirm is its only caller and is disabled in both states: `approve()` now refuses on a processing error, and a hold combined with an unpicked fee (a refusal included) returns silently instead of showing the fee warning.
