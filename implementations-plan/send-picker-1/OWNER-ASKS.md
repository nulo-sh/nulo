## 1. After a paste-to-add, which side does Send's From/To show?

- **Surface:** Send's From origin and To destination pair, after a token imported from the picker comes back picked.
- **Today, and what ships:** every token change, a row pick or this import, resets From and To to the default side (Private) when the new token supports both. Before this lane the swap also cleared the amount, so the reset went unseen. Now the import keeps the amount, as signed. Someone on Public who typed 1 comes back on Private with 1 typed, and a new token's private side usually reads "0 PICK" with "More than your balance" until they switch back.
- **Option A, keep today's reset:** no change. The person flips From to Public again, and the amount and recipient are still there.
- **Option B, the import keeps the side:** after a paste-to-add, From and To stay where the person had them when the new token supports that side. A row pick keeps today's reset, since a row pick still clears the amount.
- **Recommendation:** B. The import already keeps the amount and recipient, so keeping the side completes "the form as you left it". It touches one line beside the amount mark, plus a test.
