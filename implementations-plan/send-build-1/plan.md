# send-build-1: Send's B, "Pay a person", as signed on pages 129, 131 and 133

Send becomes B: a person step ("Who are you paying?"), then one form for that person, with the fee line pinned over Review send and a review sheet for every send. Three stacked arcs: the form, the review, the close-out. Every arc changes what users see, so none lands on its own: the orchestrator lands each on the owner's approve of its captures. No issue is linked on pages 129, 131 or 133 and none matches (`recon.md` § Open work), so no PR carries `Closes`. Recon: [`recon.md`](recon.md). Owner asks: [`OWNER-ASKS.md`](OWNER-ASKS.md); each has a "what ships now" form the arc builds without waiting, so none blocks a1 (the lane brief: "the arc ships without it").

## Outcome & Quality Bar

**For whom.** A person paying someone from the 360 × 600 popup or a 240 px side panel, by mouse, by keyboard or with a screen reader, often paying the same person again.

**What excellent looks like.**
1. Who is paid is never in doubt: the name in bold, the whole address in two lines of 33 with its start and end in bold at the line edges, and the yellow check line for an address this wallet has no record of paying. At 240 px the block wraps; nothing scrolls sideways.
2. A repeat send is two presses: the person in Recent, then Same as last time. The fee and Review send are always in view, and the fee line reads in the glossary's words, never "fee contract", "gas" or "Private FPC".
3. The keyboard reaches everything in reading order, one stop per choice: native radios for the sides, Max before the amount field (M3), the off button naming what is missing (H2). No send leaves without the armed Send now of the review.
4. Nothing the wallet does today is lost: every fee state, the Terms wall, the USD quote gate, flagged tokens, the in-flight guards and the queued-send cap behave as today.

**Good enough.** States the prototype never drew keep today's words in the nearest place (`OWNER-ASKS.md`). No new tooltip, glossary term or stored key.

## Security & Adversarial Considerations

- **Threat model.** A sender or a dApp seeding look-alike addresses; a person sent by a phishing page to "pay again". Surfaces: Recent, Same as last time, the address block, the fee payer.
- **Spoofing.** Both ends bold, the whole address, the check line for an address with no record; Recent lists only this account's popup sends that the node took, never a dApp's or an incoming sender's (D7). Names come from contacts and own accounts by exact address, never from journal titles.
- **Payer.** The warning's fix applies only the card's own `remedy`, rechecked on press, never a hand-added FPC (D5). Same as last time never sets a payer, cap or origin.
- **Sending.** One broadcast path (`submitTransfer` → `sendTxTask`); every send passes the armed Send now (D6); the Terms guard, the fiat gate at click time and the queued-fee handoff unchanged.
- **Logging.** No address, amount, name or URL in a log line; the journal read logs `{ error }` at `debug`.
- **Untouched.** Crypto, dependencies, permissions, storage.
