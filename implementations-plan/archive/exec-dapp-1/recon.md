## Collisions and context

- Open issues: none collide (`gh issue list --state open --search "sendTx wait receipt OR additionalScopes OR capsule"`). Closed `known-limit` issues: none on these paths.
- The approval window detaches at approve (`dapp-interaction/service.ts:300-310`), so a longer send does not hold it open. dApp `ExecuteOperation` tasks are not surfaced on Home (`RecentActivityView.vue` `isExecutingTask`).
- `runInSlot`'s `finally` releases the execution slot and the origin's lane place (`dapp-send-executor.ts:404-418`); the journal reads `succeeded` once `proveAndSend` returns (`execution-coordinator.ts:353`).
- The wallet-sdk transport sets no timeout on a wallet call (`@aztec-labs/wallet-sdk` `src/extension/provider/extension_provider.ts`: discovery and key exchange only).
- `WaitOptsSchema` (`@aztec-labs/aztec.js` `src/wallet/wallet.ts:330-336`) checks `timeout`, `interval`, `initialDelay`, `ignoreDroppedReceiptsFor` as plain numbers and has no `waitForStatus`; the bridge asserts the schema and passes the raw args on (`packages/wallet-bridge/src/wallet-schema-args.ts:40-60`).
- The playground comment at `transactions.ts:144-150` says a waiting send blocked on mining; at `51ea220b1` the wallet reads the receipt once, so the comment describes no current behaviour.
