# Phase 1: diagnosis and the press

- **CI evidence.** The four failed jobs (runs 44617555458, 44617747666, 44626995026 attempts 1 and 2) upload no artifacts on these lanes, and their logs show only the `openSendFeeSheet` timeout. Logs alone could not tell a lost press from a late sheet.
- **Instrumented run** (not committed): page-side capture listeners on `pointerdown`, `pointerup` and `click`, a MutationObserver on `aria-expanded`, a 10 ms box sampler on Change, and the aim point stashed by `pointerClick`. `NULO_E2E_BROWSER=firefox NULO_E2E_PROVERLESS=1 NULO_E2E_CHAOS=1 NULO_E2E_CHAOS_SEED=1960252198`, `taskset -c` to 4 cores, `send-chaos` + `same-token-concurrent-sends`: 18 tests green in 1,015 s, 44 presses, each one reaching Change. Press to open took 3-74 ms; open to payers took 12-494 ms. Aim to pointerdown took 3-85 ms (median 9). Change sat at top 491.0 (306 samples) or 506.0 (90), so each move was 15 px on a 14 px button. In 4 presses it moved within 250 ms of the press, once 39 ms after the pointerdown.
- **Red proof.** `pointer-click.test.ts` against `legal-drivers.ts` from the base: three reds. The moved case clicked only `pc-ground`, and both refusal cases resolved instead of throwing. Green on the new helper, Chrome 5.9 s and Firefox 8.1 s for the file, the browser launch included.
- **Agent shell pitfall.** zsh reads `$BASE:apps/…` as the `:a` modifier on `$BASE`. `git show $BASE:path > file` failed, and its redirect had already emptied the file. Brace the variable (`"${BASE}:path"`), and write a base copy to a scratch file before overwriting a tracked one.
