# Phase 2: Home's fixed hero over a scrolling middle

- 2026-10-16, red proof: the new Home scenario fails on the Phase 1 build for want of `home-scroller` (the hero is not separate from a scrolling middle at all).
- 2026-10-17, the middle is a block `div` scroller wrapping today's content column, not the `Flex` column itself: a column flex scroll container lets an `overflow: hidden` child shrink below its content, which today's non-scrolling column never did, so every child lays out as before.
- 2026-10-17, measured (Chrome and Firefox): hero top 86 px at rest and scrolled, shadow opacity 0 at rest and 1 once scrolled, a press in the band opens the row under it, nav bottom at the viewport's bottom at 360 × 900 and 480 × 900 (the middle scrolls 37 px on Chrome, 47 px on Firefox there), PageDown from a focused row scrolls the middle.
- Deviation: the PageDown check focuses an activity row, not a holding row. Home lists a holding only from a balance row, which the balance service reads only as it starts, so seeding one needs a background restart with the RPC held (`type-roles.test.ts`'s `openSeededHome`); the property under test, focus inside the scrolling content, is the same for either row.
- Deviation, `network/snack-placement.test.ts`: its Home case scrolled the Cancel's scroller to its end. Under the old one-page scroll the row stayed on screen there; under the fixed hero the end carries the row out of the middle's view (Chrome: Cancel's box at 262-290, under the hero, hit `home-hero`), so the step tested nothing that exists. It now scrolls the row to the top of the middle (`scrollIntoView({ block: "start" })`), the highest it shows, which is what "end" did before; every assertion stays.
- Second red, same spec, Firefox (which ran the new "top" step): every placement hit the Cancel uncovered, but the closing check `[76, 76]` failed at "centre" with 181. Centring now centres the row in the middle, low enough to sit in the snack's resting band, so the snack correctly climbs to 12 px above the Cancel (bottom 419, Cancel top 431); the old one-page centre left the row above that band. The check now states the snack's rule at every position (rest 76 px up, or 12 px above the Cancel where resting would cover it), verified against both browsers' recorded numbers, and keeps "back at rest at the top" as its own assertion.
