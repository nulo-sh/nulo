The Phase 2 sweep can surface a product error whose fix would change what a person sees (plan.md § Decision rules, R3). Each such error gets an entry here, in this form, and the arc ships without it:

- **Surface:** the screen or window, and the error the sweep recorded there.
- **Options:** each fix, with what the screen would look like after it.
- **Recommendation:** the panel's pick and why.
- **What ships now:** what the arc delivers without the answer (for example: "#326 alone; #325's recorder waits on this answer").
- **Issue:** the number of the issue that owns it.
