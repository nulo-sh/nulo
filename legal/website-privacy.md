# Website Privacy Notice

**Version 1.0 — effective 27 October 2026**

This notice explains what happens to information when you visit `nulo.sh` or join its mailing list.
The Nulo wallet itself is covered by the [Privacy Policy](privacy.md); this notice adds nothing to
what the wallet does.

> **The short version.** The website runs no analytics, no tracking and no third-party scripts, and
> sets no cookies of mine. If you join the mailing list, I keep your email address, the time you
> joined and the version of this notice then in effect. Resend stores the list and sends the emails.
> You get one email when Nulo is on the stores, then occasional product updates, and you can leave in
> one click.
>
> *This box is a summary. The sections below are the notice.*

In Argentina that processing is governed by **Law No. 25,326** (*Protección de los Datos

## 2. Visiting the website

The website is static and carries no analytics or tracking of mine.

Hosting for `nulo.sh` is provided by Cloudflare, Inc.
([privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/)). Website requests expose IP
addresses, requested URLs and ordinary browser headers to the hosting provider. Information
available to the Developer through hosting tools is aggregate traffic statistics (requests,
bandwidth, unique visitors and countries) and, for up to the past 30 days, a sample of individual
requests showing the time, IP address, country, network, user agent, method, protocol, hostname,
requested path, and how the request was cached or filtered. Request logging on the site and on the
service that receives sign-ups is turned off, and the hosting provider's browser analytics are not
enabled. This processing supports delivery and security of the site, relying on legitimate
interests where applicable.

**Retention.** These records are kept only for as long as the hosting provider makes them available
in its dashboard (currently up to 30 days); I do not export or copy them.

## 3. The mailing list

### 3.1 What I collect

When you submit the sign-up form, I keep your **email address**, the **time** you submitted it, the
**version of this notice** in effect at that time, and the fact that you joined **from the website**.
Nothing else: no name, no IP address, no wallet information.

Your address travels in the body of the request, so it does not appear in the request records of
§ 2. To limit repeated submissions, Cloudflare counts sign-ups from your IP address for about a
minute. That count is not stored with your address, and I do not log your IP address or your email
address.

### 3.2 Why, and on what basis

To tell you when Nulo is available and to send occasional product updates. **Legal basis (GDPR):**
your consent, under Article 6(1)(a). You can withdraw it at any time by leaving the list; leaving
does not affect what was sent before.

### 3.3 Who processes it

**Plus Five Five, Inc.**, which operates Resend (United States), stores the list and sends the
emails on my instructions, under its [Data Processing Addendum](https://resend.com/legal/dpa); its
subprocessors are listed at [resend.com/legal/subprocessors](https://resend.com/legal/subprocessors).
Resend stores account data, including the list, in the United States. The emails are sent from its
servers in Ireland. Resend also keeps a log of each request the sign-up service sends it, including
your address, for 30 days.

### 3.4 What you receive

One email when Nulo is available on the Chrome Web Store and Firefox Add-ons, then occasional
product updates, from `hello@news.nulo.sh`. Nothing is sent when you sign up. Replies to these
emails are not received; write to hello@nulo.sh instead.

Open and click tracking are turned off: the emails carry no tracking pixel, and their links are not
rewritten.

### 3.5 No confirmation email

I do not send a confirmation email when you sign up, so I cannot check that the address belongs to
the person who entered it. If someone enters your address, the first email you receive says why you
received it and lets you leave in one click. Before the launch email I review the list and remove
entries that are plainly not real.

### 3.6 Leaving the list, and how long I keep your address

Every email has a link that takes you off the list in one click. You can also write to
hello@nulo.sh.

I keep your address while you are on the list. When you leave, Resend keeps your address, marked as
unsubscribed, with the time you joined and the notice version, so that a later sign-up by anyone
cannot add you again. To rejoin, write to hello@nulo.sh. If you ask me to delete your address
entirely, I will, and I will tell you that a later sign-up could then add it again. Resend's request
log (§ 3.3) expires on its own after 30 days.
